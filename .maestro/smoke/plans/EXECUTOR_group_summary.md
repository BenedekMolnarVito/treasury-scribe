- PASS: the app matched the oracle.
- FAIL: the app's behaviour is wrong (an actionable bug).

- BLOCKED: the environment cannot exercise the case.
- known_red (NG16, NG17): the planner predicts a FAIL on the current HEAD because of a startup active-notification bypass.
